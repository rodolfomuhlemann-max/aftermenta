Welcome to Aftermenta
A free, open-source personal finance dashboard — inspired by Mint, built for the era after it. Upload your bank statements and understand your spending in minutes.
📋 Step-by-step guide
1
Try the demo first — no data needed
Click the green Demo mode ON button in the top right. Explore 3,000+ realistic fake transactions across every feature before uploading anything personal. This is the safest way to start and takes zero setup.
2
Export your bank statement as a CSV
Log into your online banking and download your transaction history as a CSV file — not PDF, not Excel. See the bank-by-bank instructions below. Choose any date range. The more history you include, the more useful the charts will be.
3
Upload your CSV
Click Add data in the sidebar → CSV tab → drag and drop your file. The dashboard reads it instantly inside your browser. You can upload multiple files from different accounts — they merge automatically.
4
Explore your data
Use the tabs in the sidebar:
  • Overview — financial snapshot at a glance
  • By year — click any year card for monthly breakdown and category split
  • By category — click any row to expand top merchants and full transactions
  • Transactions — full searchable, filterable history newest first
  • Budget — set monthly targets per category and track actuals
  • Savings goals — create goals and track your progress
5
Filter by time period
Use the All time / Year / Month toggle in the top right to scope every chart and table at once. All sections update together — they are fully connected in hierarchy so one filter controls everything.
6
Share your feedback
Click the green 💬 Leave feedback button in the bottom right corner. Tell me what worked, what did not, and what you would like added. This tool improves because of your input.

🏦 CIBC
  1. Online Banking → Accounts

  2. Select your account
  3. Download → CSV → select dates
🛡️ Common safety questions — answered honestly
❓ Can anyone see my financial data?
No. This dashboard runs entirely inside your browser. When you upload a CSV, it never leaves your device. There is no server, no database, no backend. Not even the creator can see your data. It is technically impossible for anyone else to access it.
❓ Is my data stored anywhere?
No. Nothing is saved. The moment you close or refresh the browser tab, all data is gone. This is intentional. Your finances are yours and should stay that way.
❓ Is there any tracking or analytics on this site?
No. No cookies, no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no tracking scripts of any kind. The only external resources loaded are Google Fonts (typography) and Chart.js (charts) — neither of these can see your financial data.
❓ What about the PDF upload feature?
One exception. The PDF extraction feature sends your PDF to the Anthropic Claude API for AI processing. If you are not comfortable with this, use the CSV upload instead — it is 100% local and does not involve any external service.
❓ Can I verify the code myself?
Yes. This is fully open source. Right-click anywhere on the page and select View Page Source — you can read every single line. The full code is also on GitHub. There is nothing hidden.
❓ Is this affiliated with any bank or financial institution?
No. This is an independent personal project, not affiliated with any bank, institution, or financial advisory service. It does not provide financial advice. All analysis is for personal tracking and informational purposes only.
